My approach
Before anything I run, a town hall, a campaign, a whole change program, I ask two questions. What business result does this accelerate, and how do we measure it?

The People function does not exist to make people feel good. It exists to help the business reach its outcomes faster, at the same or lower cost. Communication is part of that machine, not decoration on top of it. If I cannot answer both questions, I do not run the initiative yet.

Internal communications

A daily rhythm the whole company trusts

ProblemPeople were drowning in scattered updates across channels and time zones, and important news simply wasn't landing. No shared source of truth, low awareness, wasted time.
What I didBuilt and now host a company-wide daily news town hall for up to 500 people: content plan, speaker briefing, production, format.
ResultOne reliable information rhythm. Scattered updates replaced by a single trusted channel.
Team enablement

Getting a team to actually use AI

ProblemEveryone was talking about AI, but almost no one was actually using it day to day. Enthusiasm without adoption, and no way to tell whether it was making any difference.
What I didBuilt and ran an internal AI enablement program: a hands-on learning track and a redesign of how the team works.
ResultTeam AI adoption score rose from 0.92 to about 1.7 out of 4, above the quarterly target of 1.2 to 1.3. Around 2.8 new automated workflows per person, saving roughly 4 hours a week each.
Employee experience

New hires productive sooner

ProblemNew hires arrived anxious and under-informed, burning their first days on basic questions, which slowed time-to-productivity and dented the first impression of the company.
What I didRedesigned the pre-boarding flow and built a pre-boarding portal, moving part of onboarding before the start date.
ResultOnboarding time cut by about 20%. Fewer questions about start date and expectations, and new joiners arrive already excited.
The portal helped me decrease the number of questions about start date, meetings and what to expect.New hire feedback

Post-acquisition integration

Bringing two companies into one culture

ProblemAfter Muse Group and Hal Leonard came together, two organisations with different histories had to start working as one, without losing trust or momentum in the process.
What I didLed the cultural and communication integration: implemented shared culture and values, and ran town halls and workshops to bring people together across both companies.
ResultOne aligned culture and a clear, shared story through a sensitive transition.
Talent pipeline

The Open Frontend Academy

ProblemJunior frontend talent was hard to reach in Cyprus and a key role stayed open, while the employer brand had little pull with early-career engineers.
What I didSet up and ran a free two-month frontend academy: 10 students, online, with a traineeship on the Audio.com team for the top graduate.
ResultA talent pipeline and employer-brand reach that helped close an open role.
